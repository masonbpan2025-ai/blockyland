DEMO What can you tell with blocks?

From one prompt
to a short film.

These two films are a demonstration of storytelling in Blockyland. Each began as a single written request. An AI agent then wrote the screenplay, cast the voices, built the sets, directed the actors and cameras, and composed the music, all inside the game’s own engine with the same blocks and characters you play with.

Watch a film, then open “See the prompt” to read exactly what was asked for.

What the demo shows

  • StoryA screenplay with a clear arc, motivated characters and a moral, in Chinese and English.
  • VoicesA distinct neural voice for every character. Questions rise like questions, and no line is rushed.
  • ActingExpressive pixel faces, lip-sync, blinking, and bodies that walk, kneel, sit and carry.
  • SetsFarms, a workshop and a storybook frontier, built from the game’s own blocks and textures.
  • CinematographyShot lists, reaction close-ups, dissolves through time, seasons, sunsets and lighting moods.
  • SoundAn original pentatonic score that follows the story, plus footsteps, hooves, birds and crickets.

Every frame is directed with code on a fixed 24 fps clock, so the interactive preview and the finished film always match. The agent reviews its own shots and fixes what looks wrong before the final render.
