'''Cold ocean ruins''' are variants of [[ocean ruins]]. They are underwater [[structure]]s primarily composed of [[stone bricks]]. They are found in [[ocean]] biomes and rarely on [[beach]]es, and are inhabited by [[drowned]] and sometimes [[zombie nautilus]]es alongside them. They contain [[chest]]s and [[suspicious gravel]] that contain a variety of [[loot]], most notably buried [[treasure map]]s, exclusive [[pottery sherd]]s, [[nautilus armor]], and [[emerald]]s.

== Generation ==
Cold ocean ruins generate in normal, cold, and frozen ocean biomes or in their deep variants. They are composed of stone materials and contain some suspicious gravel blocks that can be excavated using a brush. 

Ocean ruins may at times be at least partially buried, including with their airspaces. During generation, all air blocks and structure blocks act as structure voids. This means that their associated suspicious blocks are also located under layers of usual gravel, and even stone. 

Suspicious gravel randomly replaces normal gravel during generation, and are not part of the structure definition. 

Although most ocean ruins generate underwater, they sometimes generate on land, bordering a beach or another seaside biome. 30% of ocean ruins generate as large ocean ruins; the other 70% generate as small ruins. If the ocean ruin is large, there is a 90% chance for 4-8 (inclusive) small ocean ruins to generate around it in a cluster. The "brick" variant of the chosen structure type generates with a structure integrity of 0.9 if large and 0.8 if small. The "cracked" variant generates with a structure integrity of 0.7 and the "mossy" variant generates with a structure integrity of 0.5, both regardless of size. Reduced structure integrity may also remove drowned and zombie nautiluses from the structures.

== Structure ==
{{main|/Structure}}
The shape and size of cold ocean ruins varies greatly, and they often generate together in groups to create ruined villages.

All but two of the structures contain a loot [[chest]] with loot inside. The chest contains loot for specifically either a small or large sized ocean ruin structure and may be buried under blocks such as [[gravel]].

Cold ocean ruins are primarily composed of [[stone bricks]], [[gravel]], and [[suspicious gravel]], and they also contain [[prismarine]], [[sand]], [[magma block]]s, [[sea lantern]]s, [[polished granite]], [[glazed terracotta]], [[bricks]], [[planks]], [[cobblestone]], [[mossy cobblestone]], [[stone brick stairs]], [[obsidian]], or a [[chest]] with loot in it.

Natural cold ocean ruins generate following the rules above and may not exactly match the stored structures.

The structures below are located in the folder <code>[[client.jar]]/data/minecraft/structures/underwater_ruin</code>. The following is a complete table of the structures with their filenames, descriptions and what they consist of.

Each {{BlockLink|Structure Block|structure block}} indicates either a chest with random loot or the spawn location of a drowned/zombie nautilus [[mob]].

Upon world generation, many of the structures spawn with [[drowned]] (usually one or two, though a few structures have three or four). These drowned are persistent, and do not despawn. Adult drowned wielding tridents may also also spawn riding a [[zombie nautilus]] as a zombie nautilus jockey.

Zombie nautiluses spawned from ruins are also persistent and will even spawn on [[Peaceful]] difficulty, despite being [[monster]]s, as the mobs spawned from ocean ruins count as part of the structure and ignore conventional spawning mechanics, leaving the zombie nautilus spawning on Peaceful by itself.<ref>{{bug|MC-304858||Zombie nautiluses can spawn on peaceful by themselves, unlike skeleton/zombie horses and camel husks}}</ref>
