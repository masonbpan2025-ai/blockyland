'''Warm ocean ruins''' are variants of [[ocean ruins]]. They are underwater [[structure]]s primarily composed of [[sandstone]]. They are found in [[ocean]] biomes and rarely on [[beach]]es, and are inhabited by [[drowned]] and sometimes [[zombie nautilus]]es alongside them. They contain [[chest]]s and [[suspicious sand]] that contain a variety of [[loot]], most notably buried [[treasure map]]s, exclusive [[pottery sherd]]s, [[nautilus armor]], [[emerald]]s, and [[sniffer egg]]s.

== Generation ==
Warm ocean ruins generate in warm, lukewarm, and deep lukewarm ocean biomes, and are composed of sandstone and stone materials as well as having some blocks of [[suspicious sand]] within them. 

Ocean ruins may at times be at least partially buried, including with their airspaces. During generation, all air blocks and structure blocks act as [[Structure void|structure voids]]. This means that their associated suspicious blocks are also located under layers of usual [[sand]], [[gravel]], and even stone. Suspicious sand randomly replaces sand respectively during generation, and are not part of the [[structure definition]].

Although most ocean ruins generate underwater, they sometimes generate on land, bordering a [[beach]] or another seaside [[biome]].

30% of ocean ruins generate as large ocean ruins; the other 70% generate as small ruins. If the ocean ruin is large, there is a 90% chance for 4-8 (inclusive) small ocean ruins to generate around it in a cluster. Large warm ocean ruins generate with a [[structure integrity]] of 0.9 and small warm ocean ruins generate with a structure integrity of 0.8. Reduced structure integrity may also remove [[drowned]] and [[Zombie nautilus|zombie nautiluses]] from the structures.

Warm ocean ruins can be located with warm ocean ruins [[explorer map]]s, which are found in secret chests in [[abandoned camp]]s.

== Structure ==
{{main|/Structure}}
The shape and size of warm ocean ruins varies greatly, and they often generate together in groups to create ruined villages.

All but two of the structures contain a loot [[chest]] with loot inside. The chest contains loot for specifically either a small or large sized ocean ruin structure and may be buried under blocks such as [[sand]].

Warm ocean ruins are primarily composed of [[sand]], [[sandstone]], and [[suspicious sand]], and they also contain [[polished granite]], [[polished diorite]], [[sea lantern]]s, [[magma block]]s, [[gravel]], [[terracotta]], [[sandstone stairs]], or a loot [[chest]].

Natural warm ocean ruins generate following the rules above and may not exactly match the stored structures.

The structures below are located in the folder <code>[[client.jar]]/data/minecraft/structures/underwater_ruin</code>. The following is a complete table of the structures with their filenames, descriptions and what they consist of.

Each {{BlockLink|Structure Block|structure block}} indicates either a chest with random loot or the spawn location of a drowned/zombie nautilus [[mob]].

Upon world generation, many of the structures spawn with [[drowned]] (usually one or two, though a few structures have three or four). These drowned are persistent, and do not despawn. Adult drowned wielding tridents may also also spawn riding a [[zombie nautilus]] as a zombie nautilus jockey.

Zombie nautiluses spawned from ruins are also persistent. They even spawn on [[Peaceful]] difficulty despite being [[monster]]s, because they count as part of the structure and ignore conventional spawning mechanics.<ref>{{bug|MC-304858||Zombie nautiluses can spawn on peaceful by themselves, unlike skeleton/zombie horses and camel husks}}.</ref>
