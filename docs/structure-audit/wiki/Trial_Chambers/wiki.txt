'''Trial chambers'''{{note|The name is inconsistently plural in-game:

* <code>trial_chambers</code> in-game ID
* "Step foot in a Trial Chamber" — "Minecraft: Trial(s) Edition" [[advancement]] {{in|java}}
* "Trial Chambers" — the in-game structure name {{in|bedrock}}.
}} are uncommon underground [[structure]]s that serve as a mid-game combat challenge.<ref>{{Mcnet|minecraft-snapshot-23w45a|Minecraft Snapshot 23w45a|November 8, 2023}}</ref> They consist mostly of [[copper]] blocks and [[tuff bricks]], and are the only place where [[trial spawner]]s and [[vault]]s naturally generate. Trial chambers are also the only place where [[breeze]]s spawn, whose [[breeze rod]]s can be used to craft [[wind charge]]s. Various materials such as [[heavy core]]s and certain [[armor trim]]s are obtained exclusively by unlocking vaults in trial chambers. As it is the only source of breeze rods and heavy cores, the structure is also the only location where [[mace]]s can be obtained.

Trial chambers can also be used to obtain [[copper bulb]]s without entering [[the Nether]], and large quantities of copper blocks and their derivatives.

Most copper blocks found in trial chambers are waxed, preventing them from [[oxidizing]]. However, some unwaxed copper blocks can also generate due to a bug.

[[Ominous trial]]s can also take place here through the [[Bad Omen]] effect. Bad Omen converts to [[Trial Omen]], which causes all trial spawners to become [[ominous trial spawner|ominous]], making them more difficult and providing greater rewards.

== Generation ==
Trial chambers generate underground in the [[Overworld]]. The starting room generates at an [[altitude]] of between Y=-40 and -20, but the structure is not restricted to those Y levels; the majority of the structure's rooms tend to generate between Y=-20 and 0.{{verify}}

The generation of trial chambers follows a grid of 34×34 [[chunk]] regions centered on the world origin (X=0, Z=0). There is always one trial chambers structure generated in each region at a random location, unless a deep dark biome cancels the generation of the trial chambers.

While the origin point of the structure ({{code|corridor_end}}) cannot generate within a deep dark biome, other parts of it can intersect a deep dark biome.

Trial chambers are one of the only structures that generate on the same locations on ''Java Edition'' and ''Bedrock Edition''.

=== Locating ===
Trial chambers can be located using [[buried trial chambers map]]s, which can be obtained in two ways.
* Buried trial chambers maps are sold by journeyman-level [[cartographer]] villagers for 12 [[emerald]]s and a [[compass]]. The maps always point to the nearest trial chamber relative to the cartographer that does not already have a buried trial chambers map that points to it. All maps bought from the same cartographer always point to the same structure.
* Buried trial chambers maps can be found in loot of secret chests in [[abandoned camp]]s. (see {{slink|Abandoned Camps|Secret chests}}).

Trial chambers are buried underground, so exposed caves will usually have weird shapes that will determine the location of a trial chamber.

{{IN|je}}, a given villager always trades the same map, which is determined upon the trade unlocking.

== Structure ==
{{Main|/Structure}}
[[File:Trial Chambers layout.png|thumb|Overhead view of a typical trial chamber layout.]]
The starting room of the structure is {{cd|corridor/end}}. This room connects to a chamber and a section of a corridor. At the end of the section of a corridor, an intersection links to another chamber and another section of a corridor is generated, but this time it ends on the atrium, which then leads to the entrance room.

The structure then branches out from some {{cd|corridor/straight}} pieces.

=== Components ===
==== Corridors ====
[[File:Trial Chambers corridor diagram.svg|thumb|A diagram depicting every piece that constitutes a corridor.]]

Trial chambers are composed of two corridors:
* a corridor going from the entrance room to the intersection, passing through four slices and the atrium;
* a corridor going from the intersection to the end room, passing through four slices.

The intersection connects both corridors, forming an L-shape. The intersection and the end room always lead to a chamber (see {{slink||Chambers}}), while slices have a probability to lead to a hallway (see {{slink||Hallways}}).

===== Entrance =====
[[File:Trial Chamber entrance doorway.png|thumb|The doorway found in all entrance rooms.]]

The entrance generates at the end of one of the two corridors, passing through the atrium. Each entrance contains a "doorway" that consists of waxed oxidized copper blocks, including its cut copper and chiseled copper variants.

<code>entrance_1</code> and <code>entrance_2</code> include a [[vault]], two entrance chests, and a platform containing either an [[oak]], a [[mangrove]], or a [[cactus]]; additionally, <code>entrance_1</code> has a secret room containing a bed and a chest.

<code>entrance_3</code> includes two vaults and three entrance chests.

===== Slices =====
[[File:Example Hall.png|thumb|A door leading from a corridor to a hallway.]]
[[File:Trial Chambers Vibrant Visuals.png|thumb|A corridor viewed from the ground.]]

Corridor slices, or straight pieces,{{note|The template pool is named <samp>slice.json</samp>, while the structures are named <samp>straight_N.nbt</samp>.}} are sub-structures that make up the corridors; they are generated on top of a tuff brick plate. Each of the two corridors has four slices, composed randomly.

Except for <code>straight_1</code> and <code>straight_8</code>, all slices have a copper door:
* <code>straight_2</code> and <code>straight_5</code> generate a copper door on the lower level;
* <code>straight_3</code> and <code>straight_6</code> generate a copper door on the middle level;
* <code>straight_4</code> and <code>straight_7</code> generate a copper door on the upper level.

On average, each trial chamber has 6.29 ({{frac|44|7}}) doors. These copper doors can either lead directly to a chamber, a hallway or nothing.<ref name="deadend">{{bug|MC-266451||Trial Chamber door leads to nothing/a stone wall|WF}}</ref> Provided the latter case doesn't happen:
* lower level doors always lead to a descending spiral staircase ({{frac|1|2}} chance between <code>lower_hallway_connector</code> or <code>trapped_staircase</code>);
* upper level doors always lead to an ascending spiral staircase (<code>upper_hallway_connector</code>);
* middle level doors and these spiral staircases have a 97.48% chance to directly lead to a chamber, 2.44% chance to lead to a hallway piece, and 0.08% chance to directly lead to a dead-end (see {{slink||Hallways}});
* any doors in any level, especially the spiral staircases leading to them in the case of upper and lower level doors, can also generate dead-end pieces as fallback pieces.

Both <code>straight_6</code> and <code>straight_8</code> generate with a [[trial spawner]] that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob (see {{slink||Mobs}}), but always never [[breeze]]s. <code>straight_7</code> generates with a hidden [[vault]].

Slices can generate with various decorations such as [[flower pot]]s, [[decorated pot]]s, [[candle]]s and corridor [[barrel]]s (see {{slink||Decor}}). Slices can have addons, some of them generate with a trial spawner (that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob, but never breezes), a dispenser trap or a vault. Some slices cannot contain specific types of or all corridor addons.

===== End =====
The end room generates at the end of one of the two corridors. It always leads to one of these chambers with equal probability:
* <code>chamber_1</code>
* <code>eruption</code>
* <code>assembly</code>
* <code>slanted</code>

End rooms cannot lead to any other chamber, i.e. it excludes:
* <code>chamber_2</code>
* <code>pedestal</code>
* <code>chamber_4</code>
* <code>chamber_8</code>

End rooms always include an [[ominous vault]]. In addition to its ominous vault, <code>end_2</code> also features a regular [[vault]] and an chest with intersection loot.

==== Intersections ====
[[File:Trial Chamber Bedroom.png|thumb|A bedroom within an intersection.]]
[[File:Trial Chamber intersection_2 secret room.png|thumb|The secret room in <code>intersection_2</code>.]]

* <code>intersection_1</code> and <code>intersection_2</code> feature an [[ominous vault]];
* <code>intersection_2</code> and <code>intersection_3</code> feature a [[barrel]] with loot;
* <code>intersection_2</code> features a secret room with an additional bed and a [[decorated pot]] always containing 3 [[string]]s.<ref name="string">{{bug|MC-272087||In Trial Chambers, Decorated Pot in "intersection_2" doesn't have a loot table}}</ref>

==== Hallways ====
{{Missing information|section|{{BE}} weights due to encounters not existing on this version of the game; all weights listed in this section are for {{JE}}.}}

[[File:Trial Chamber hallway.png|thumb|A hallway viewed from the ground.]]

Hallways are segments that connect corridors to chambers. When a copper door generates in a corridor, it (or, in the case of an upper or lower door, the spiral staircase that follows it; see {{slink||Slices}}) may lead to one of the following pieces:
* a chamber (97.48%);
* a hallway segment (1.14%);
* an encounter (0.41%);{{only|java}}
* a dead end (0.08%).

If the selected piece cannot be generated, the game attempts to generate a different piece. After a certain number of failed attempts, it defaults to generating a dead end.{{info needed|How many attempts does it take for the fallback pool to be called?}} If the dead end itself cannot be generated, no piece is generated.<ref name="deadend"/>

This process is then repeated at the end of each hallway segment or encounter. Furthermore, hallways never branch, meaning that every copper door found in corridors eventually leads to a single chamber, a dead end, or nothing.

==== Chambers ====
{{Missing information|section|{{BE}} weights due to encounters not existing on this version of the game; all weights listed in this section are for {{JE}}.}}

Chambers are large combat rooms that contain multiple trial spawners and vaults, as well as one ominous vault. Chambers can be accessed in numerous ways:
* they have a chance to generate in corridor slices (chambers can generate straight from slices by middle level copper doors) and hallways, in this case, all chambers can generate;
* they can always generate in intersections, also in this case, all chambers can generate;
* additionally, {{cd|assembly}}, {{cd|chamber_1}}, {{cd|eruption}} and {{cd|slanted}} can generate in the end of corridor ends.

Each chamber design has several potential entrances, of which only one is connected to the rest of the structure. The remaining entrances are sealed with an entrance cap (see {{slink||Miscellaneous}}). Each potential entrance, including the sealed ones, generates a disposal consisting of a hopper placed above a barrel (see {{slink||Decor}}), allowing players to manage their inventory before entering a chamber.<ref name="disposal">{{tweet|kingbdogz|1819127158813725021|All those empty chests you saw in Trial Chambers (which were for organizing and throwing away unwanted loot) are now hoppers + barrels instead:|August 1, 2024}}</ref>

== Mobs ==
{{anchor|Mob spawning}}
Natural mob spawning do not occur within the trial chambers. Instead, mobs can spawn in the trial chambers through [[trial spawner]]s or unnatural mob spawning sources.
;Mobs from trial spawners
{{Main|Trial Spawner#Mob spawning}}

These mobs above spawn from the [[trial spawner]]s. The number of mobs spawned scales with the number of players in range of each spawner, and breezes are spawned less per spawner and per player than other mobs.

When a trial chamber generates, one mob from each of the following categories is randomly selected for the trial spawners in that trial chamber. Every mob within the same category has an equal chance to be selected. There are four (or five if the slow ranged category is counted) categories for trial chamber mob spawning.
* Breeze (always spawn a [[breeze]])
* Melee ({{frac|1|3}} chance to spawn either a [[husk]], a [[spider]] or a [[zombie]])
* Ranged and slow ranged ({{frac|1|3}} chance to spawn either a [[bogged]], a [[skeleton]] or a [[stray]])
* Small melee ({{frac|1|4}} chance to spawn either a [[baby zombie]], a [[cave spider]], a [[silverfish]] or a [[slime]])
Not all trial chambers will spawn with all four categories. However, the breeze category is guaranteed to spawn in all trial chambers.

*Dispensers can also contain [[egg]]s, which has a chance to spawn chickens.

== Trivia ==
* The idea of trial chambers evolved from [[CornerHard]] pitching in a meeting to revisit the [[monster room]].<ref>{{twl|clip=y|KawaiiArbitraryNikudonKeyboardCat-BsrnVON_L8vehSz8}}</ref>
* The design of trial chambers was inspired by the architecture of {{w|Mesoamerica}}n civilizations, whose geometric designs fit well into ''Minecraft''. The design combines the aesthetic of ancient civilizations with a futuristic feel.<ref>{{ytl|bBr8BBrk1v8|ALL ABOUT THE TRIAL CHAMBERS|Minecraft|t=97s}}</ref>
* A scrapped chamber design appears in two [[YouTube]] videos showcasing the trial chambers and the trial spawners.<ref>{{ytl|lD7twuq1AoU| Minecraft 1.21: Early peek at trial chambers @ 0:40|Minecraft|November 16, 2023|t=40}}</ref><ref>{{ytl|FpE6q0nvVjA| Minecraft 1.21: A closer look at trial spawners @ 0:30|Minecraft|November 17, 2023|t=30}}</ref> It includes a trial spawner, two chests, and two redstone lamps with levers on top.
* [[Brandon Pearce]] thinks that whoever created trial chambers in-universe seem to be "pretty tinkery".<ref>{{tweet|kingbdogz|1819129051249872907|I think whoever or whatever built the Trial Chambers seems to be pretty tinkery - it doesn't feel much far off from what is already done with dispensers and traps in the structure.|August 1, 2024}}</ref>
* There are around 4,500 copper blocks in an average trial chamber. If these were all mined and converted to copper ingots, this would be approximately 40,000 copper ingots.
