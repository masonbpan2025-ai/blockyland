# 1500 Cartoon Players: Three Islands, Three Weddings
### 1500名卡通玩家：三座岛，三场婚礼 · storyline for review

**Length:** about 10 minutes · **Audience:** 8–11 · **Narrator:** the playful Pocoyo-style narrator
**Tagline:** *Three islands. Three couples. One very long server.*

The same server as *1500 Players Simulate Civilization*: 1,500 cartoon players, three islands, 500
players each. This time the story follows three couples from the day they meet to the day their
children host their own martial-arts tournament, and ends with a fourth love story: Goku and
Chi-Chi's son Gohan and Videl. Gohan first appears after his parents' wedding.

| Island | Couple | Where they live |
|---|---|---|
| Temperate Island (green fields) | **Goku & Chi-Chi** | Chi-Chi's carrot farm by Buu's Bakery, and the Central Plateau (the hill of the lantern night, the wedding and the tournament) |
| Hot Island (desert mesas) | **Vegeta & Bulma** | Bulma's Capsule Corp dome, Vegeta's mesa |
| Cold Island (snow and ice) | **Krillin & Android 18** | Krillin's camp, Frieza's Ice Palace, MerCat's grotto |

**Supporting cast:** Yamcha (Bulma's old friend), Frieza (the villain, later the tournament
announcer), Majin Buu (baker), Dr. Briefs, Pocoyo, Pato, Elly and Loula, Gabby, Pandy, Cakey and
DJ Catnip.
**New characters:** Chi-Chi, Android 18, and the next generation: Gohan and Goten (Goku and
Chi-Chi), Trunks and Bulla (Vegeta and Bulma), Marron (Krillin and 18), and Videl, the young
champion of the Temperate Island, who falls for Gohan.

### What it teaches (without saying it out loud)
Each lesson is shown by a character choosing it, not lectured.

- **Love starts with respect.** Goku likes Chi-Chi because she's strong and fair, not because she's
  pretty. Bulma won't help anyone who is rude to her, prince or not.
- **Keep your promises, and a real apology changes something.** Goku forgets their date. Saying sorry
  is easy; tying a knot in his belt so he never forgets again is what fixes it.
- **Tell the truth, even when you're proud.** Vegeta hides a mistake, and the secret hurts Bulma more
  than the broken machine did.
- **Love is never control.** Krillin holds the remote that could switch 18 off. He breaks it, because
  he wants her to choose.
- **Friends don't jump to conclusions, and friends can be happy for you.** Yamcha blames Vegeta,
  apologises, and ends up cheering at the wedding.
- **Marriage is a team.** The vows are about listening, sharing chores and cheering for each other's
  dreams.
- **Friendly competition.** The kids fight hard, shake hands, and ask for a rematch.
- **Friendship first, no deals.** Videl offers to keep Gohan's secret in exchange for flying lessons.
  Gohan teaches her anyway: friends don't trade favours for secrets.
- **Love grows up, too.** Gohan and Videl start as friends who learn from each other. The younger kids
  tease them, and they stay kind about it. Years later they marry under the same arch as Gohan's
  parents.

Kids that age often say "ewww" about love stories. The narrator says it first, and the film wins them
over with fights, jokes and cake.

## Act I · Meeting (Days 1–7, borders locked) · 0:00–2:50

**1 · Cold open: the lobby** (0:00)
1,500 players spawn in a white void. The narrator says: "This is a story about... love!" A crowd of
cartoon kids groans "Ewww!" The narrator adds: "...and kung fu, and robots, and a VERY big cake."
The crowd cheers.

**2 · The map** (0:25)
Three islands, three portrait pins, one for each couple: "Nobody here has met yet. That's about to
change."

**3 · Temperate Island: the carrot thief** (0:45)
- Goku is starving, so he pulls a carrot from a farm. A girl in a purple dress flips him onto his back
  in one move. It's Chi-Chi.
- Chi-Chi: "That's MY carrot." Goku, from the dirt, delighted: "Wow! You're strong! Fight me again
  tomorrow?" Chi-Chi: "Only if you pay for the carrot."
- He pays by ploughing the whole field before lunch.
- Running joke: Chi-Chi tells her friends, "If he ever beats me, I'll marry him." Goku: "What's
  'marry'? Is it a kind of food?"

**4 · Hot Island: "Say please"** (1:30)
- Vegeta lands in Bulma's lab: "Build me the strongest training machine on this server."
  Bulma: "Say please." Vegeta: "A prince does not say please." Bulma: "Then a prince doesn't get a
  machine."
- She doesn't mind his pride; she minds him being rude.
- Running gag: Vegeta tries to say it ("Pl... pll... PFFT"). Yamcha, Bulma's old friend and lab
  partner, watches him and frowns.

**5 · Cold Island: cocoa at the gate** (2:10)
- Krillin, shivering at his camp, carries two mugs of hot cocoa to the Ice Palace gate. Frieza's new
  head of security stands there: Android 18.
- 18: "I'm an android. I don't need cocoa." Krillin: "Everyone needs cocoa." She ignores him.
- Next morning the mug is empty. Every morning after, two mugs.

## Act II · Friends, and trouble (Days 8–14, borders open) · 2:50–5:50

**6 · Day 8: the Three-Island Fair** (2:50)
- The world border opens, and everyone sails to Buu's Bakery for the first fair. The six meet.
- Goku and Vegeta size each other up and start a sparring rivalry on the spot.
- Bulma and Chi-Chi become friends: "You invented a FLYING car?" / "You flipped a SAIYAN?"
- 18 says she came "for the cookies". (She is secretly on a mission for Frieza.)

**7 · Twist 1: the empty lantern** (3:30)
- Goku promised Chi-Chi he'd come to the Lantern Festival on the plateau hill.
- He spars with Vegeta "just one more round"... thirty-one times. Chi-Chi waits on the hill with two
  paper lanterns until the last one goes out.
- Next morning Goku brings her a huge plate of food. Chi-Chi: "I don't want food, Goku. I want you to
  keep your promises."

**8 · A real apology** (4:05)
- Goku, honest: "I forgot because fighting is fun. But you're more important than fun. I'm sorry."
- He ties a knot in his belt: "Now I'll never forget." He keeps it in every scene after.
- Chi-Chi forgives him, and they plough the field together.

**9 · Twist 2: the broken machine** (4:30)
- Bulma's Gravity Machine bursts in a cloud of smoke in the night.
- Yamcha: "I saw Vegeta sneaking in last night!" Everyone stares at Vegeta. He says nothing, too proud.
  Bulma, hurt: "Get out of my lab."

**10 · The camera never lies** (5:00)
- Baby Box plays back his security camera. Vegeta cracked the machine while training, and spent all
  night secretly trying to fix it before anyone noticed.
- Vegeta: "I broke it. I wanted to fix it before you knew." Bulma: "I can fix machines, Vegeta. I
  can't fix secrets. Next time, just tell me."
- Yamcha apologises for blaming him without asking: "...Sorry, man." Vegeta: "Hmph. Accepted."

**11 · Twist 3: the spy** (5:25)
At night 18 slips Bulma's blueprints into her jacket. Krillin sees her and can't believe it. He doesn't
shout. He asks: "Are you okay?" She flies away to the Ice Palace without answering.

## Act III · The Big Freeze (Days 15–19) · 5:50–7:40

**12 · Day 15: the Big Freeze** (5:50)
- Frieza builds a freeze tower from the stolen blueprints. The sea between the islands freezes, snow
  falls on the farms, and the carrots turn to ice.
- On every screen Frieza announces: "From now on, there is ONE island. Mine."
- Banner: THE BIG FREEZE.

**13 · Five heroes, one table** (6:15)
The first time they all work together (all but 18, who is still with Frieza), round a map table outside Bulma's dome:
- Bulma knows how to switch the tower off, but someone has to get inside.
- Chi-Chi organises every farmer on the island to save the seeds and feed everyone.
- Goku and Vegeta must fight side by side (both: "With HIM?!").
- Krillin: "I'm going to get 18."

**14 · The remote** (6:45)
- In the throne hall Frieza holds up a remote: "One click and my android switches off." Krillin grabs
  it, and it lands in his hand. 18 freezes, waiting for him to press it.
- He snaps it in two. "Nobody should have a remote for someone they care about. You choose."
- 18 looks at him for a long second, then turns to Frieza: "I choose cocoa."

**15 · Teamwork beats the freeze** (7:05)
- 18 types the shutdown code, Bulma talks her through it on the radio, and Goku and Vegeta hold off
  Frieza's soldiers back to back. Chi-Chi drives the tractor full of farmers to clear the frozen
  bridge.
- The tower bursts into a shower of snowflakes and the sea melts.
- Frieza lands in a snowdrift. Pocoyo offers him a hand up and an invitation: "Wedding? Cake?"
  Frieza: "...Front row."

## Act IV · Three proposals, one wedding (Day 20) · 7:40–8:40

**16 · Three proposals** (7:40), a quick, funny montage:
- **Goku:** "Chi-Chi, I know what 'marry' means now. It's not food. It means I show up, every day.
  Will you?" (Then: "...Is there food at the wedding?")
- **Vegeta:** he builds a firework machine that writes "MARRY ME" in the sky, then can't say it out
  loud. Finally: "Bulma... marry me. ...PLEASE." This pays off the running gag. Bulma: "You said
  it!"
- **Krillin:** two mugs of cocoa, and the ring is in hers: "Every morning, for the rest of our lives?"
  18: "...Fine. But you're making it."

**17 · The triple wedding on the Central Plateau** (8:05)
- Under a cherry-blossom arch on the plateau (where, years later, the kids will build their ring): Pato officiates, Elly is the flower girl, Pocoyo carries the rings on a
  cushion, and Buu baked a cake as tall as a house.
- Twist: Loula steals the ring cushion, and the whole wedding chases the dog round the plateau.
- The vows, one line each:
  - Chi-Chi and Goku: "I promise to listen." / "I promise to show up."
  - Bulma and Vegeta: "I promise to tell the truth." / "...And to say please."
  - 18 and Krillin: "I promise to choose you every day." / "I promise to never stop making cocoa."
- Frieza cries in the front row.

## Act V · The next generation · 8:40–10:00

**19 · Server Year 15: the kids want a tournament** (8:40)
- Time skip card: SERVER YEAR 15. Goku and Chi-Chi have two sons: Gohan (14, always reading) and
  Goten. Trunks, Bulla and Marron play across all three islands: snowball fights on Cold Island, sand
  forts on Hot Island.
- Trunks and Goten argue about who is the strongest kid on the server. Marron: "Then let's settle it
  properly." The kids decide to host the first Tenkaichi Budokai.
- Gohan writes the rule book: "A tournament needs rules. No flying out of the ring, and shake hands
  after."

**20 · Gohan meets Videl** (8:55)
- Videl, the island's young champion, signs up and is sure of one thing: Gohan is the mysterious
  Great Saiyaman, the helmeted hero who keeps rescuing cats from the bakery roof.
- Twist: the Saiyaman helmet falls out of Gohan's bag. He admits it.
- Videl: "Teach me to fly, and I'll keep your secret." Gohan: "I'll teach you anyway. Friends don't
  need deals."

**21 · The flying lesson** (9:10)
- On the plateau, Videl wobbles a hand's breadth into the air, arms out. Gohan stays close in case she
  falls.
- Goten, Trunks and Marron pop up from behind a rock: "Gohan has a GIRLFRIEND!" Gohan, bright red:
  "She's my *friend*." Videl, floating past: "...For now."

**22 · The Tenkaichi Budokai** (9:25)
- The kids build a stone ring on the Central Plateau, where their parents married. Gohan referees and
  Frieza is the announcer.
- The Junior final is Goten vs Trunks, then a twist: Marron. While the boys show off with flashy
  poses, Marron calmly steps between them and nudges both out of the ring at once. Marron wins!
- Goten, from the grass: "Good match! Again tomorrow?" It's his dad's line from Scene 3. Everyone
  shakes hands.

**23 · The exhibition match** (9:35)
Goku vs Chi-Chi. She flips him onto his back, exactly like the day they met. Goku, lying in the ring,
laughing: "Best. Fight. Ever."

**24 · Server Year 22: the fourth wedding** (9:45)
- Under the same cherry-blossom arch, Gohan and Videl marry. The three original couples and all the
  kids flank the aisle. Chi-Chi cries; Goku asks if there's food; Vegeta, arms folded, says "Hmph.
  ...Congratulations." (He said it. Bulma notices.)
- **Moral:** 爱不是赢，是每天都出现、说真话，让你爱的人自由地选择你。
  *Love isn't winning. It's showing up, telling the truth, and letting the people you love choose you.*
