# 1500 Players Simulate Civilization · new ending: *The Invasion*

**For review.** Acts I and II stay as they are. Act III changes completely: the Doomsday border is gone. On day 15, thousands of angry little aliens invade the server. All three islands fight them together and win. In the middle of the war, Gohan and Videl save each other. The film ends with their wedding, the biggest the server has ever seen.

## What changes

| | Now | New |
|---|---|---|
| Day 15 | The sky turns purple and the world border starts closing | The sky turns green: 500 saucers and 3,000 angry Grumblers arrive |
| The villain | Frieza attacks with Death Balls | Frieza invited the aliens to conquer the server for him. They take *his* palace first, and he ends up fighting beside the heroes |
| The climax | A Spirit Bomb turned into a dome of light | Gohan and Videl crack the mothership's shield together, and Goku's Spirit Bomb sends it spinning home |
| Gohan and Videl | Study partners at the cat café | Study partners → battle partners who save each other → married |
| The ending | 42 survivors on the last floating block | Nobody is left behind. Everyone is at the wedding, even the littlest alien |
| Length | about 10:00 | 13:04 as voiced: the acted OpenAI voices run slower than the old ones, and Act III is 5:28 (trims are suggested at the end) |

One small change earlier in the film sets up the payoff. In the café (Act I), Videl asks *"Can you teach me to fly someday?"* and Gohan says *"Sure! Anytime."*. In Act III she can't fly yet, so she rides the Flying Nimbus to catch him.

## The aliens: the Grumblers

- **Who they are:** little round green aliens in the simple, toy-like style of Pocoyo's world. They have big heads, big eyes under cross brows, one antenna with a glowing tip, and silver space suits with a purple belt. The design is ours; no show's characters are copied.
- **How many:** 3,000 Grumblers in 500 flying saucers, plus one mothership the size of a town, with one giant angry eye underneath. The eye is the shield generator.
- **Commander Grumbleflux:** three eyes, purple armour, a red cape and a three-pronged antenna crest.
- **Zib:** the littlest Grumbler, and the only one who smiles. He misses his saucer home, and Pocoyo invites him to the party.
- **Why they're angry:** Grumblers are always grumpy; it's in the name. Frieza told them the server was theirs for the taking.
- **Keeping it gentle for 8–11 year olds:**
  - Their goo rays don't hurt. They stick you in green jelly, like a gummy bear, until your friends pull you out.
  - The heroes knock down saucers, never people.
  - At the end the Grumblers aren't destroyed. They're beaten, and they fly home.

## What the new ending teaches

- **Teamwork across differences.** Three islands that spent two weeks competing win only when they fight as one. Even Goku and Vegeta agree: "just this once".
- **Partners look after each other.** Gohan catches Videl; Videl catches Gohan. Neither one is the rescuer; they take turns, which is a healthy relationship.
- **Choices come back to you.** Frieza invites someone to take other people's things, and they take his first.
- **Kindness after a fight.** The war is won, then Pocoyo invites the littlest enemy to the party. A callback to Act II: "Nobody ever invites Pocoyo. He just comes."
- **The moral, said once at the end:** *A good team isn't the one that never falls. It's the one that catches each other.*

On screen: the whole archipelago under a sky full of saucers, with an **INVASION** banner.

### 17 · Twist: who invited them? (7:30)

> NARRATOR: *And who sent them the invitation?* 是谁给他们发的邀请呢？
> FRIEZA: *Grumblers! Conquer this server for me, and I shall be your king!* 格兰博星人！替我征服这个服务器，我就当你们的国王！
> COMMANDER: *Thank you for the invitation, Frieza. We'll start with your palace.* 谢谢你的邀请，弗利萨。我们就先从你的宫殿开始。
> FRIEZA: *That was NOT the deal!* 这可不是我们说好的！
> NARRATOR: *Never invite someone to take other people's things. They might take yours first.* 千万别邀请别人去抢别人的东西。他们可能先抢你的。

On screen: the mothership's tractor beam lifts Frieza's ice palace, block by block. It's a world broadcast, so everyone sees it.

### 18 · One plan for three islands (8:00)

> NARRATOR: *Everyone ran to the Central Plateau. Hot, cold and temperate. Together.* 大家都跑到了中央高原。炎热岛、寒冷岛、温和岛，一起。

> VEGETA: *Kakarot. Just this once... we fight together.* 卡卡罗特。就这一次……我们一起战斗。
> GOKU: *Yeah! That's the spirit!* 好啊！就是这样！
> GABBY: *Carlita, walls! Pink ones!* 卡莉塔，筑墙！粉红色的！ (the pink barricade goes up round the plateau)
> PATO: *Fine. I will wear the armour. It goes with my beak.* 好吧，我穿盔甲。跟我的嘴巴挺配的。 (a callback to Act I)
> FRIEZA: *Nobody takes my world but me. Move over, monkeys.* 除了我，谁也别想抢我的世界。让开，猴子们。
